# Shadow Studio v0.35.0

- Preview selectable web captions in the built-in player.
- Open the caption timeline in Create or Review Library and click a cue to seek.
- Long captions split into shorter, two-line cues within the recorded narration.
- Move dialogue lines up or down before rendering. Audio settings, pauses, and effects move with the line.

Research references for this release: [Viblo](https://viblo.ai/), [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/help/how-to-recognise-subtitles), and [OpusClip](https://faq.opus.pro/). Shadow Studio uses its own renderer and local narration timeline.
